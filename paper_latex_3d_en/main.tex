\title{Multi-Relation Role Graph Reinforcement Learning for Heterogeneous UAV Kill-Chain Recovery under Limited Communication and Intermittent Sensing}
\author{To be completed}

\begin{document}

\begin{abstract}
Heterogeneous unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) teams must close cooperative kill chains under incomplete sensing, limited communication, and temporary link or platform degradation. A key challenge is that the platforms contributing to the kill chain play different roles: a scout may detect the target, a relay may maintain information flow, and an attacker may form the final engagement window. Treating all interactions as a single homogeneous graph can obscure whether an edge corresponds to perception, communication, or task support. This paper proposes EA-RG-MAPPO-S, a multi-relation edge-aware role graph multi-agent reinforcement learning method for 3DOF heterogeneous UAV cooperative interception. The method constructs separate perception, communication, and dynamic task-support relations, and performs role-pair-conditioned message propagation under centralized training and decentralized execution. A staged topology curriculum exposes the policy to communication range variation, dropout, message delay, radar dropout, and temporary communication-node failure. Experiments in a constrained 3DOF cooperative interception environment show that the proposed method improves relay-failure kill-chain recovery compared with a matched single-graph MAPPO variant. Formal ablations indicate that dynamic task-support relations and role-pair message gates contribute to the recovery behavior. A strict-sensing pilot further shows that the relay-failure recovery advantage remains when true target-state fallback is removed from observations.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} heterogeneous UAVs; multi-agent reinforcement learning; multi-relation graph; limited communication; intermittent sensing; cooperative interception; kill-chain recovery
