\begin{abstract}
Heterogeneous unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) teams must close cooperative kill chains under incomplete sensing, limited communication, and temporary link or platform degradation. A key challenge is that the platforms contributing to the kill chain play different roles: a scout may detect the target, a relay may maintain information flow, and an attacker may form the final engagement window. Treating all interactions as a single homogeneous graph can obscure whether an edge corresponds to perception, communication, or task support. Moreover, even when a team eventually succeeds, it may take many steps to re-establish effective coordination after a node or link failure. This paper proposes EA-RG-MAPPO-S, a task-graph-driven multi-relation multi-agent reinforcement learning method for 3DOF heterogeneous UAV cooperative interception. The method encodes task-dependency, communication availability, and state-dependent relations into a multi-relation graph, and processes them with state-dependent, edge-feature-modulated attention under centralized training and decentralized execution; a structured role-pair initialization prior (Gate Prior) and a static role-pair modulation are used as architectural components. In a locked three-seed held-out evaluation (10{,}800 episodes; strict-sensing relay-failure setting), the method achieves near-saturated recovery reliability ($0.971 \pm 0.021$, three training seeds) while reducing post-failure recovery latency by approximately 38\%, 34\%, and 59\% relative to MAPPO, HAPPO, and a parameter-matched graph baseline (mean recovery times 10.8 vs.\ 17.4 / 16.3 / 26.2 steps; the smallest seed-level variance). Gate Prior notably improves cross-seed optimization stability (cross-seed gate correlation 0.962 vs.\ 0.562 without it), and removing the task-support relation degrades held-out recovery and slows recovery, indicating an empirical contribution. The role-pair modulation shows no consistent independent benefit and is retained only as an auxiliary component.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} task graph; multi-relation coordination; post-failure recovery; multi-UAV semantic search; multi-agent reinforcement learning; limited communication; intermittent sensing
