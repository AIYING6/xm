\begin{abstract}
Heterogeneous unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) teams must close cooperative kill chains under incomplete sensing, limited communication, and temporary link or platform degradation. A key challenge is that the platforms contributing to the kill chain play different roles: a scout may detect the target, a relay may maintain information flow, and an attacker may form the final engagement window. Treating all interactions as a single homogeneous graph can obscure whether an edge corresponds to perception, communication, or task support. This paper proposes EA-RG-MAPPO-S, a multi-relation edge-aware role graph multi-agent reinforcement learning method for 3DOF heterogeneous UAV cooperative interception. The method constructs separate perception, communication, and dynamic task-support relations, and performs role-pair-conditioned message propagation under centralized training and decentralized execution. In a strict-sensing relay-failure setting where decentralized actors do not receive global target-state fallback, the proposed method improves post-failure kill-chain recovery over no-graph and single-graph baselines under a five-seed fixed-budget evaluation. The full method achieves an 88.6\% recovery rate, compared with 53.2\% for the single-graph baseline and 21.8\% for the no-graph baseline, while maintaining zero test collisions. Mechanism ablations show that removing role-pair-conditioned message gating significantly degrades recovery, and removing task-support relations lowers mean recovery with stronger seed-level heterogeneity. Failure-aligned analysis further shows that the proposed method recovers the kill chain more consistently after relay failure despite sharp communication-connectivity degradation.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} heterogeneous UAVs; multi-agent reinforcement learning; multi-relation graph; limited communication; intermittent sensing; cooperative interception; kill-chain recovery
