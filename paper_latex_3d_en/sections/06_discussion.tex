\section{Discussion}

The current evidence supports a focused claim: multi-relation role graph learning improves relay-failure kill-chain recovery in the constrained 3DOF task. The result should not be overstated as full superiority under all communication and sensing conditions. Nominal straight-target success intervals overlap, range-compression stress is mixed, and maneuvering-target pilots still have low absolute success.

The strict-sensing pilot is important because it removes target-state fallback from observations. However, it used only 10 PPO fine-tuning updates. It strengthens the relay-failure story but should remain labeled as scenario-depth evidence unless a longer strict-sensing training budget is later completed.

The current 3DOF environment is a practical bridge toward 6DOF replay, missile models, radar models, and human-UAV teaming. Those extensions should be treated as future validation layers rather than requirements for the first Q2-targeted submission.
