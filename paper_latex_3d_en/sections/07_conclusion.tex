\section{Conclusion}

This paper presents EA-RG-MAPPO-S, a multi-relation role graph reinforcement learning method for heterogeneous UAV kill-chain recovery under limited communication and intermittent sensing. The method separates perception, communication, and task-support relations and uses role-pair-conditioned message propagation under centralized training and decentralized execution.

In a strict-sensing 3DOF relay-failure task, the proposed method improves fixed-budget post-failure recovery over no-graph and single-graph baselines. Across five training seeds and 500 matched test episodes, the full multi-relation method achieves 88.6\% recovery, compared with 53.2\% for the single-graph baseline and 21.8\% for the no-graph baseline, while maintaining zero test collisions. Mechanism ablations show that removing role-pair-conditioned message gating significantly degrades recovery, timeout, and failure-window tracking. Removing task-support relations also lowers mean recovery, although with stronger seed-level heterogeneity.

The current result should be viewed as a 3DOF mechanism foundation rather than a complete operational air-combat system. Future work will extend the evidence chain toward stronger scenario-depth tests, including maneuvering targets, jammer or escort interference, and limited 6DOF replay validation with higher-fidelity missile and radar models.
