\section{Conclusion}

This paper presents EA-RG-MAPPO-S for heterogeneous UAV kill-chain recovery under limited communication and intermittent sensing. The method uses perception, communication, and task-support relations with role-pair-conditioned message propagation, and it is trained with a staged topology curriculum. In the current 3DOF evidence chain, the strongest result is relay-failure recovery: the multi-relation policy improves recovery probability and reduces recovery time compared with a matched single-graph variant. Formal task-support and role-pair-gate ablations support the mechanism, while a strict-sensing pilot provides additional scenario-depth evidence. Future work will extend the current platform toward stronger maneuvering-target curricula, 4v2 red-blue scenarios, and LAG/JSBSim replay.
