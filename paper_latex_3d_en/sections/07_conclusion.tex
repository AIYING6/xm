\section{Conclusion}

This paper presents EA-RG-MAPPO-S, a task-graph-driven multi-relation reinforcement learning method for heterogeneous UAV kill-chain recovery under limited communication and intermittent sensing. The method encodes task-dependency, communication availability, and state-dependent relations into a multi-relation graph and processes them with state-dependent, edge-feature-modulated attention under centralized training and decentralized execution; a structured role-pair initialization prior (Gate Prior) stabilizes role-structured optimization, and a static role-pair modulation is retained as an auxiliary component.

In a locked three-seed held-out evaluation (10{,}800 episodes, strict-sensing relay-failure setting), the method maintains near-saturated recovery reliability ($0.971 \pm 0.021$). A pre-specified censor-aware survival analysis on the matched-exposure scenarios yields a primary restricted mean survival time of $14.47 \pm 3.10$ steps, lower than MAPPO ($20.39 \pm 7.72$) and the wider single-graph baseline ($16.49 \pm 8.64$) but close to HAPPO ($14.14 \pm 2.94$); the full-horizon recovery is therefore competitive rather than uniformly superior. The most consistent advantage is concentrated in the early post-failure period: at $\tau = 80$, corresponding to the active node-failure interval, EA-RG reduces RMST from $15.51$ to $11.81$ steps relative to MAPPO, with all three training seeds in the same direction. Ablations and parameter-trajectory analysis provide strong evidence that Gate Prior improves cross-seed optimization stability (cross-seed gate correlation 0.962 vs.\ 0.562) and worst-seed behavior. Removing the task-support relation degrades held-out recovery and slows recovery, indicating an empirical contribution whose exact temporal mechanism remains inconclusive. The static role-pair modulation provides limited independent benefit and is therefore not presented as a core contribution.

A pre-specified zero-shot OOD evaluation (protocol v1.1, 8400 episodes) delimits the boundary of this advantage: it is partially preserved under geometry shifts, reversed under communication-topology shifts (MAPPO attains higher recovery and lower RMST80 when the longest blue--blue edge is pruned), and overwhelmed by maneuver and joint shifts in which all compared methods saturate the recovery endpoint. The seven-cell equal-weight Full-minus-MAPPO RMST80 aggregate is $+2.565 \pm 5.567$ steps with a bootstrap 95\% interval of $[-2.362,\ +8.435]$ and $P(\Delta<0)=0.175$ (Gate~C). EA-RG therefore provides reproducible early post-failure recovery gains under the nominal held-out distribution, but this advantage is distribution-dependent under zero-shot OOD shifts.

The current result should be viewed as a 3DOF mechanism foundation rather than a complete operational air-combat system, and the OOD result defines where the method's early-recovery benefit can and cannot be expected. Future work will extend the evidence chain toward stronger scenario-depth tests, including more training seeds, an external communication baseline, and OOD shifts that target specific architectural components (e.g., topology-preserving communication patterns) to test whether the reversal under communication-topology pruning can be mitigated, with higher-fidelity missile and radar models.
