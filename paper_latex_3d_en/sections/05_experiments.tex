\section{Experiments}

\subsection{Experimental Protocol}

The current formal comparison uses matched single-graph and multi-relation checkpoints under relay and scout communication-node failure. Each formal node-failure scenario uses three training seeds and 30 independent evaluation episodes per checkpoint-scenario pair. The strongest claim is intentionally narrow: relay-failure kill-chain recovery.

Figure~\ref{fig:recovery_summary} shows the main recovery evidence and the relay-failure mechanism ablations. Under relay failure, EA-RG-MAPPO-S improves post-failure recovery from 92.2\% to 100.0\% and reduces recovery time from 21.8 to 5.6 steps. Removing dynamic task-support relations or role-pair message gates degrades relay-failure recovery.

\subsection{Strict Intermittent Sensing Pilot}

The strict-sensing setting removes true target-state fallback from local observations, shared observations, and graph target nodes before valid detection. Existing node-failure curriculum checkpoints are fine-tuned for 10 PPO updates under strict sensing, then evaluated with 30 episodes per seed and scenario. This is reported as a budget-labeled scenario-depth pilot.
