|---|---|---|---|
| P1_CORE | Failure-time task-chain recovery, and earlier RMST80 recovery of EA-RG than MAPPO in locked nominal held-out evaluation | Title, Abstract, Introduction, Fig. 2, Table 1, Results 5.1, Discussion, Conclusion | A condition-specific comparative finding, not universal superiority. |
