# T6 Family B — Role-Specific Support Response

## Question

This family tested whether support-state action separation has a consistently stronger GOOD-than-WEAK interaction for every role. Such a result would have supported a role-specific target.

## Result

| Role | GOOD separation | WEAK separation | GOOD minus WEAK |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Scout | 0.2315 | 0.1130 | +0.1186 |
| Relay | 0.1398 | 0.1568 | -0.0171 |
| Attacker | 0.2321 | 0.1594 | +0.0727 |

The frozen Family B rule required a meaningful interaction and consistent GOOD-over-WEAK direction across every role. Relay violates the rule. Therefore **Family B FAIL**.

## Consequence

The data do not justify naming one role, adding a role gate, or proposing a role-specific support-response mechanism. Scout and attacker signals cannot erase the relay counterexample.
