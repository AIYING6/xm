# UTR final verdict

`UTR_BENCHMARK_PLUS_SIMPLE_BASELINE_POSITIONING_FEASIBLE`

UTR is feasible for publication as a transparent, structured topology-randomization baseline
inside a legally and physically specified redundant-topology UAV benchmark. It is **not**
feasible to position UTR itself as a novel general MARL algorithm.

The next permitted scientific decision is external-comparator design and approval; this audit
does not authorize comparator implementation, training, new seeds, evaluation, or automatic
continuation.
