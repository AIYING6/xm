# T2 Mechanism Decision

## Immutable evidence boundary

This decision uses only the completed T1 reference's source-closed native raw
telemetry and offline derivatives. It changes neither the historical results
nor the S2 environment, information boundary, rewards, UTR-SG architecture,
PPO settings, seed identities, or evaluation data.

## Decision rationale

The frozen GOOD (`2202`, `2204`) and WEAK (`2203`, `2205`) labels show a common
failure-aligned pattern in F0, timing `28/80`, and duration `44/120`:

1. both WEAK seeds have zero pre-event mean `chain_support_t`, while both GOOD
   seeds have positive support and better attacker target approach;
2. immediately after failure onset, GOOD retains legal target information and
   nonzero direct-path use, while WEAK has zero means for both; and
3. through the middle/late windows, WEAK retains less support/legal
   information and ends farther from the target with poorer task progress.

The signal is descriptor-paired, repeats across both GOOD/WEAK seeds and all
three designated failure families, and occurs before the terminal window. It
is not reducible to direct link availability or generic action smoothness.

However, the pre-event difference is substantial and seed 2204 demonstrates
that a high timeout rate alone does not determine return quality. The existing
telemetry therefore does not identify a failure-event-only causal mechanism or
the unique learning defect that creates it. A causal-style M1 conclusion would
overstate observational development evidence.

## Single permitted target for later method design

**PRIMARY_ALGORITHMIC_TARGET:** actor-legally inferable **task-support
continuity through a topology switch**—specifically, preserving or promptly
reconstructing legal target-information support after relay-node-induced path
reconfiguration without privileged failure/topology truth.

This names the measured target, not an algorithm. It authorizes neither a
network, loss, memory module, graph modification, curriculum, or training
run. Any later proposal must show how it acts only on frozen legal actor
inputs, why it distinguishes itself from existing controls, and why it tests
this target rather than merely increasing capacity or changing exposure.

## Stop rule

T2 is complete. No new data, rollout, tape, training, held-out evaluation,
canonical seed, algorithm implementation, or historical-method revival is
authorized by this decision.
