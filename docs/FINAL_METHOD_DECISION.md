## 2. Reusable compute

The Phase-D 2M UTR trajectories for development seeds 2002 and 2101–2104 are complete, strict-continuous, configuration-matched development comparators. If a future candidate exactly preserves the frozen S2 environment/reward/PPO/actor boundary and fixed 50% nominal + six-group uniform exposure, these five UTR runs should be reused rather than retrained. They remain development-only, not formal-paper evidence. Details are recorded in [EXISTING_COMPUTE_REUSE_AUDIT.md](EXISTING_COMPUTE_REUSE_AUDIT.md).

## 3. Real unresolved problem

There is a real **descriptive system-performance problem**: UTR-SG 2M OOD-worst ranges from 36.046 to 116.450 across five training seeds, and weak cells are timeout-heavy. The frozen task is technically valid: trigger success within the alive-at-onset risk set is 1.0, constraints remain zero, and pre-trigger collisions are retained as policy safety outcomes.

There is **not** a real, isolated, algorithm-specific problem supported by the available evidence. The current actor already receives legal local graph relation, communication, task-support, age and confidence features. Episode aggregates show performance/topology associations but do not establish whether the remaining seed dispersion comes from graph representation, temporal aliasing, optimization, critic coupling, or task competence.

No sole candidate survives. Consequently **`FINAL_METHOD_PRETRAINING_DOSSIER.md` is intentionally not created**: that document is permitted only for `STRONG_GO_FINAL_METHOD`.

## 5. Closest prior art and novelty boundary

The nearest research families already cover edge-aware message passing, GNN-MAPPO with communication limits/attrition, dynamic communication graphs, communication-constrained priors, common-information/belief representations, and multi-agent world models. The reviewed primary-source matrix is in [FINAL_METHOD_PRIOR_ART_AND_CANDIDATE_REVIEW.md](FINAL_METHOD_PRIOR_ART_AND_CANDIDATE_REVIEW.md).

No candidate has an exact mathematical object that is both absent from these families and compelled by the present empirical gap. Therefore no defensible “exact novelty,” final mechanism, or result-free algorithm contribution exists at this time.

## 7. Paper contribution and reviewer-risk assessment

The evidence already supports a bounded system-robustness contribution under R1:

1. a legally audited heterogeneous UAV communication–task setting with relay-node-induced path reconfiguration rather than artificial blackout;
2. paired nominal/failure and topology/path/safety evidence with preserved information-boundary claims;
3. a transparent record that parameter-matched graph architectures and adaptive training ideas were screened without suppressing unfavorable seeds.

It does **not** support an algorithm paper centered on a speculative extra module. A reviewer could correctly characterize each screened candidate as either an existing module applied to UAVs, an unsupported combination, or a mechanism trained before its premises were verified.

## 8. Final decision

```text
NO_STRONG_METHOD_FOUND
```

This is a convergence result, not a declaration that the scientific problem is invalid. It means the present zero-training evidence does not justify spending GPU compute on another algorithm-design experiment. Preserve R1 as the active paper backbone; retain all negative routes and the existing UTR compute as provenance. Any future change of direction requires a new author-approved research question and a fresh pre-training evidence contract—not a continuation, rebranding, or minor modification of DRTP, TCR, EDR, RSG, or the screened families.
