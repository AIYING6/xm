# T5 Final Decision

## D — NO_GO

T5 reviewed exactly one task-support utilization mechanism and rejects it before implementation or training.

The frozen T4 result remains intact: good and weak policies differ in their sensitivity to already legal task-support evidence, particularly around topology perturbation. T5 does **not** convert that correlational utilization gap into a causal response law. The sole candidate required good policies to exhibit a more topology-consistent support→action response from pre-onset to early post-onset. The offline result is the reverse in aggregate (`0.846` good versus `0.881` weak; good−weak `−0.036`). Role-specific response structure was also not established by the dedicated test.

In addition, the closest primary literature makes ordinary conditioning, feature modulation, input-gradient regularization, and policy invariance insufficiently distinct for a Strong-Q2 contribution. Without the empirical response-law prerequisite, even a Solid-Q2 method would amount to adding an unvalidated regularizer.
