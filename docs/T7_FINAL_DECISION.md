# T7 Final Decision — Calibrated Support-Sensitivity Method Design

## Decision

**D — NO_GO**

The algorithm-development route closes here under T7's anti-loop rule.

## Evidence chain

1. T6 identifies broad actor-legal support utilization as a moderate,
   observational development target; it does not assert that sensitivity should
   be maximized.
2. T7's only lawful executable reference, local support quality, fails its
   pre-specified calibration premise after matching: seed ordering fails,
   timing has a negative GOOD-minus-WEAK direction, F0 lacks the weak matched
   comparison, and no matched transition-window support exists.
3. Without a reference, a finite-difference TVD objective is indistinguishable
   in substance from ordinary conditional policy learning or generic
   sensitivity/input-gradient regularization.
4. The three required reviewer attacks are not defeated. The absence of a
   supported calibration law is a scientific barrier, not a lack of tuning.

## Frozen boundaries

- T1–T6 conclusions remain unchanged, including T5 `NO_GO` and T6 `D2`.
- No T7 final method dossier is generated because neither `STRONG_Q2_GO` nor
  `SOLID_Q2_GO` is justified.
- No implementation, training, rollout, tape, held-out evaluation, canonical
  seed, reward/PPO/environment modification, or new mechanism discovery is
  authorized by T7.

## Allowed interpretation

The project has credible evidence that existing UTR policies differ in their
use of legal task-support information. It does **not** have evidence for a
specific causal, publishable calibration intervention. This result prevents a
generic regularizer from being presented as a topology-robust MARL innovation.
