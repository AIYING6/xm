# DRTP 6-UAV V3 sustained-support Q0 contract

V3 is an independent, unreleased task family. It preserves the six-agent role
layout and discrete action alphabet, but makes relay forwarding a capacity-one
policy decision. A terminal can advance only under same-transition legal
support for its assigned objective. The role, topology and action contracts are
identical for any future UTR and DRTP comparison.

Q0 runs only an information-privileged scripted feasibility witness. Its
purpose is to verify environment semantics, not to estimate learnability or
method performance. A Q0 pass authorizes at most a separate UTR-only pilot.
It does not authorize DRTP training, method comparison, parameter selection or
manuscript performance claims.
