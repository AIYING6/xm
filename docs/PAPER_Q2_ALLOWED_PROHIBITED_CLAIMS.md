# PAPER-Q2 Allowed and Prohibited Claims

**Generated:** 2026-08-26. This document is a hard writing boundary. DRTP/UTR paired results are the paper's primary algorithm comparison; SNR remains an internal mechanism audit and is not the main comparative table. Historical conclusions remain archived and visible, not rewritten.

## Allowed

- Relay failure induces legal communication-path/topology reorganization and measurable mission degradation in the frozen task.
- UTR and DRTP use a matched single-graph backbone, a nominal exposure anchor, predefined topology perturbation groups, and differ only in conditional perturbation weighting.
- DRTP shows high average gains in some formal/development cohorts while also showing reproducible adverse seeds; both sides are part of the primary evidence.
- SNR is retained as an internal fixed-nonuniform audit that limits the interpretation of “adaptive is necessary”; it is not the paper's main comparator.
- Seed-level reliability, safety, trigger validity, and adverse seeds are part of the result, not anomalies to hide.
- The mechanism is topology/path reorganization and coordination/task-support degradation, not mandatory information loss.

## Prohibited

- “DRTP/SNR is seed-stable,” “reliably superior,” “consistently outperforms,” or “robust for every seed.”
- “UTR is universally optimal,” “UTR is seed-stable,” or any claim beyond the frozen task and prospective cohort.
- “Adaptive weighting is necessary,” or a claim that the historical DRTP gain establishes a general algorithmic benefit.
- “Relay is the necessary or unique information mediator.”
- “Failure causes information blackout/loss” or “DRTP restores lost information.”
- Strict recovery guarantee, universal topology generalization, or deployment guarantee.
- Pooling 3M development and 10M held-out results as one homogeneous experiment.
- Treating a ratio such as `J_OOD/J_F0` as an absolute-superiority hard gate.
- Hiding seed1902 development NO-GO, held-out seed2002 reversal, or safety reversals.
- Reusing old EA-RG recovery claims, old Gate1 tables, or legacy multi-relation figures as DRTP evidence.
- Using SNR's internal audit as if it were a new main-paper algorithm comparison or deleting it from the provenance record.
