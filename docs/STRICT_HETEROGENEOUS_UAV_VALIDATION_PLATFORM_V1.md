# Strict heterogeneous UAV validation platform V1

Status: `DYNAMIC_CAPABILITY_RESPONSIBILITY_LINE_CLOSED__NO_CTRR__NO_NP2`

The current repository is frozen as a validation platform, not an algorithm
incubator.  Its maintained assets include:

* 3DOF heterogeneous Scout/Relay/Attacker simulation;
* strict recipient-specific actor information contract;
* legal sensing, delivered/cache-valid communication, range, dropout, delay,
  and age semantics;
* continuous guidance and independent physical neutralization endpoint;
* MAPPO infrastructure, paired evaluation, checkpoint/hash/provenance gates;
* deterministic construct, boundary, and failure-diagnosis tooling.

The following lines are closed and may not be revived by renaming or adding
minor modules: EA-RG v2, acquisition-conditioning M0/M2R, hybrid-action
collapse as a new algorithm problem, and dynamic-capability responsibility
reallocation/CTRR.

Historical results remain useful for engineering and audit context, but are not
automatically evidence for a future algorithm paper.
