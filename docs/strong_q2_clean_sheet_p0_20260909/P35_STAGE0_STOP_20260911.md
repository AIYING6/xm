# P35 Stage-0 decision-relevant belief handoff: STOP

## Scope

P35 was a zero-training qualification candidate for a capacity-limited,
multi-event confirmation-and-response task. It was not an environment, a
training run, or a method-performance experiment.

The frozen contract and executable audit are respectively:

- `configs/p35_decision_relevant_belief_handoff_stage0_20260911.json`
- `scripts/p35_decision_relevant_belief_handoff_stage0_audit.py`
- `artifacts/diagnostics/p35_decision_relevant_belief_handoff_stage0_20260911/P35_STAGE0_RESULT.json`

## Frozen result

The audit enumerated 6,561 abstract two-event decision states. Four of the
five predeclared gates passed:

- confirmation changed the best response event in 74.93% of states;
- all four unique action families occupied at least 10.05% of feasible
  states, with neither direct-response family dominant;
- confirmation beat the best direct response in 44.93% of states, while a
  direct response beat confirmation in 55.07%; and
- the utility contains no probe bonus, action-label reward, or scripted
  priority.

However, the evidence-age gate failed. Across 473 posterior-bin/window
comparison groups containing at least two evidence ages, the unique optimal
action family changed in only 8.46%, below the predeclared 10% threshold.

`P35_STAGE0_STOP` is therefore the correct result.

## Consequence

P35 must not be converted into an environment, a learned policy method, or a
training line. The result does **not** show that confirmation or active
information acquisition is generally unhelpful. It shows only that, under
this frozen abstract construction, evidence age is not sufficiently
decision-material to justify a paper whose central mechanism requires it.

No post-result tuning of priors, likelihood ratios, utilities, retention, or
time windows is authorized.
