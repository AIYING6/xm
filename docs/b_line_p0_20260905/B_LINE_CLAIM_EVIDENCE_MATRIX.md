| Future claim | Direct evidence required | Metric / decision rule | What does **not** support it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Current topology snapshot is insufficient | Paired same-`(X_t,A_t,M_t)` instances with legal history difference | preferred action differs under frozen semantics | A single high mission score |
| Transition state changes the decision | Deterministic counterexample and later instance-level audit | feasibility and lexicographic preference | A generic recurrent policy result |
| A future solver improves mission outcome | Matched deterministic baselines on common instances | mission success/degradation | Toy counterexample alone |
| Benefit is transition-specific | snapshot-only versus transition-aware ablation with matched objective | paired utility and feasibility delta | System-level average only |
| Safety/connectivity is preserved | Independent hard-feasibility ledger | violations, outage duration, recovery latency | Mission gain that offsets violations |
| Scaling is practical | 3/6/8-UAV and graph-density suite | runtime, memory, worst-case cost | One small handcrafted instance |

System competitiveness, module attribution, and safety are intentionally distinct future claims.
