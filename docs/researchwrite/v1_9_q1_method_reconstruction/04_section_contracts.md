# v1.9 Design Contracts

## A. Method contract: PCRF

- **Purpose:** represent a receiver-local conflict between legal evidence
  sources, not a generic graph capacity increase.
- **Inputs:** source-tagged direct perception and delivered/cache-valid packet
  claims, plus source-free legal self/task context.  Age/confidence are C-only;
  no merged target cache may enter common context.
- **Allowed claims:** the gate changes factor weighting under measurable legal
  disagreement; the construction cannot reveal unavailable teammate truth.
- **Forbidden claims:** online “fault adaptation,” universal superiority,
  static Role-Pair novelty, information fields not shared by comparators, or
  being first to separate perception/communication, fuse conflict, or handle
  stale messages.
- **Validation:** R5-style leakage suite passes; parameter counts are matched;
  gate changes correlate with conflict descriptor rather than hidden global
  state; neutral behavior is checked under agreement.

## B. Primary comparison contract

- **Purpose:** test pure architectural value.
- **Methods:** PCRF vs parameter-matched wider single graph, with identical
  recipient-specific raw actor information, packet/cache semantics, optimizer,
  budget, seed set, and selector.
- **Allowed claim:** earlier establishment only if the frozen three/five-seed
  criterion, hierarchical paired bootstrap, and no major establishment-rate
  tradeoff all hold.
- **Forbidden claim:** attributing a MAPPO/HAPPO difference to PCRF structure.
- **Validation:** raw feature hashes/masks match across graph methods; selected
  checkpoints come from immutable validation records only.
  T2MAC, CDCMA, CoDe, Communication-Aware UAV MARL, and AsynCoMARL are
  mandatory first-tier related-work comparators for the eventual manuscript.

## C. Secondary comparison contract

- **Purpose:** test whether graph aggregation, not information privilege,
  contributes.
- **Methods:** PCRF vs matched-information non-graph legal pooling.
- **Allowed claim:** a secondary representation advantage; it cannot replace a
  failed primary comparison.
- **Validation:** equal raw actor information and comparable parameter budget.

## D. Mechanism diagnostic contract

- **Purpose:** establish relation-conflict exposure and the predicted gate /
  representation response.
- **Inputs:** all pre-frozen P/C scenarios: agreement, fresh P/stale C,
  P-unavailable/C-valid, C-unavailable/P-valid, and relay-failure conflict.
- **Allowed claim:** diagnostic consistency with the mechanism.
- **Forbidden claim:** selecting only favorable conflict cells or treating this
  suite as nominal primary evidence.
- **Validation:** all cells and seeds reported; no EA-RG-only input field.

## E. OOD contract

- **Purpose:** test a pre-specified robustness prediction after nominal core
  evidence is established.
- **Allowed claim:** severity-dependent robustness only when all mild/medium/
  strong levels and the pre-specified joint cells are reported.
- **Forbidden claim:** “robust” from one non-saturated favorable cell.

## F. Change-control contract

- **Purpose:** prevent result-driven redesign.
- **Rule:** once v1.9 formal training begins, architecture, information
  contract, seeds, budget, selector, primary comparison, endpoint, and
  confirmatory anchor are immutable. Any change starts a new version.
