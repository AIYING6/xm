# M0 — Final Decision

## Decision

`A — MATURE_METHOD_GO`

`PRIMARY_FINAL_METHOD = TC-SAM-UTR`

## Basis

TC-SAM-UTR is the only candidate that simultaneously passes actor legality, preserves the frozen SG architecture and UTR exposure, has direct policy-optimisation precedent, stays within the preferred approximately 2× training cost, avoids the already observed high-gain feedback loops, and remains defensible as a targeted Q2 adaptation rather than a bare application.

The alternative graph-smoothing premise is contradicted by M0’s recorded-state probe; local adversarial graph augmentation overlaps the existing topology exposure and risks semantic ambiguity; the information-bottleneck family is not a minimal matched-SG adaptation. These routes are closed for this project.

## Historical status

DRTP held-out reversal, TCR catastrophic instability, support-utilisation route closures, and `C — EDR_DEV_FAIL` remain unchanged. M0 does not reinterpret any prior result.

## Cost estimate

The eventual one-shot five-seed development comparison requires one baseline UTR arm and one TC-SAM-UTR arm at a common frozen endpoint. TC-SAM-UTR is expected to cost about twice a normal PPO update; compared with a UTR-only five-seed reference, the total paired programme is therefore roughly three baseline-equivalent five-seed budgets. This cost is not authorised by M0.

## Stop rule

M0 authorises only a separately reviewed final design/implementation contract for TC-SAM-UTR. It does **not** authorise implementation, training, new tape generation, held-out evaluation, canonical seeds, or Rank 2/3 training. If TC-SAM-UTR later fails a proper paired five-seed experiment, algorithm development ends permanently.
