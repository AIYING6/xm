# DRTP journal shortlist

This is a scope-based shortlist, not a promise of a current JCR quartile. Verify the active category, JCR release and special-issue status immediately before submission.

| Tier | Candidate | Submission condition | Why it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stretch | *Robotics and Autonomous Systems* | PLR and 6-UAV results both support the main story, with clear algorithmic analysis and reproducibility package | Its stated scope includes advances in robot control and learning for autonomous systems. |
| Primary | *Journal of Intelligent & Robotic Systems* | Core A/B and OOD evidence remain clear; cross-scale study is complete | It explicitly includes a special section on unmanned systems and is a natural robotics-systems audience. |
| Safety net | *Drones* (AI in Drones / swarm-intelligence fit) | UAV framing, system details, reproducibility and final evidence package are all polished | The journal explicitly covers UAV design/application, AI/ML and UAV swarm intelligence. |

Before selecting a venue, compare the completed PLR and 6-UAV results against the claim--evidence matrix, then choose the highest tier whose claim scope the evidence actually supports. Do not target a venue by quartile label alone.

Official scope pages: [Robotics and Autonomous Systems](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/journal/09218890), [Journal of Intelligent & Robotic Systems](https://link.springer.com/journal/10846), and [Drones](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/drones/about).
