# Figure 3 provenance and boundary\n\n**Source:** `drtp_stabilization_A_complete_results.tar.gz` and `drtp_stabilization_B_complete_results.tar.gz`, both SHA256-verified before analysis.\n**Inputs:** five final DRTP sampler logs per cohort; no checkpoints or episode-evaluation tape were loaded by the figure script.\n**Unit:** training seed; cohorts are plotted separately and never pooled.\n\nPanel A/B show the logged non-nominal group probability `q` at five normalized update milestones. The line is the cohort mean and the band is the range across five seeds. Panel C relates each seed-group's final logged nominal-relative difficulty to its realized share of non-nominal reset selections. It is descriptive process evidence only; it does not establish an internal causal mechanism or a root cause of a particular seed outcome.\n\nThe final sampler manifests provide failure-group and timing semantics but no explicit graph adjacency/edge list. This figure therefore does not show broken-edge counts, graph connectivity, or path-length statistics.\n
