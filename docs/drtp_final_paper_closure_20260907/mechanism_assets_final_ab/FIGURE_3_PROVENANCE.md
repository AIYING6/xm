# Figure 3 provenance and boundary\n\n**Source:** `drtp_stabilization_A_complete_results.tar.gz` and `drtp_stabilization_B_complete_results.tar.gz`, both SHA256-verified before analysis.\n\n**Inputs:** five final DRTP sampler logs per cohort; no checkpoint or episode-evaluation tape was loaded by the figure script.\n\n**Unit:** training seed; cohorts are plotted separately and never pooled.\n\nPanels A/B of `Fig3_DRTP_topology_exposure_evolution` show the logged non-nominal group probability `q` at five normalized update milestones. The line is the cohort mean and the band is the range across five seeds. Panel C relates each seed-group's final logged nominal-relative difficulty to its realized share of non-nominal reset selections. It is descriptive process evidence only; it does not establish an internal causal mechanism or a root cause of a particular seed outcome.\n\nThe final sampler manifests provide failure-group and timing semantics but no explicit graph adjacency/edge list. Figure 3 therefore does not show broken-edge counts, graph connectivity, or path-length statistics.\n\n## Supplementary Fig. 3b: endpoint difficulty versus realized exposure\n\n`Fig3b_topology_group_difficulty_vs_exposure` is generated from `DRTP_FINAL_TOPOLOGY_DIFFICULTY_SUMMARY.csv`. Each point is one failure group aggregated across five training seeds within cohort A or B. Its horizontal axis is the fixed-endpoint mean nominal-relative return gap and its vertical axis is the logged training-period realized non-nominal exposure share. It is a descriptive alignment check, not a graph-statistics analysis and not causal evidence that an endpoint gap uniquely determines exposure or final policy performance.\n
