# 2701/2702/2703 contrast

DRTP 2701 is favorable on every final condition; 2702 is adverse on every
condition; 2703 is favorable or near-neutral except D120 (-1.00 J). 2702 is
therefore a useful contrast case, but it is not a replicated class of failure.

Notably, q distance is not unique to 2702: DRTP 2703 has a larger mean q L1
than 2702 in 0.25–0.50M (0.695 versus 0.602) without an equivalent final
reversal. This directly limits any claim that q deviation alone explains H2.
