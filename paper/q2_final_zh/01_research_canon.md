# 01 Research Canon

## A. Frozen facts

1. The scientific event is relay-node failure followed by legal communication-path and task-support reconfiguration.
2. A legal direct Scout-to-Attacker path may remain; complete information blackout is not established.
3. UTR-SG-MAPPO and DRTP-SG-MAPPO use the same 116,728-parameter Single-Graph MAPPO backbone.
4. Both use the same PPO, seven topology groups, 50% nominal anchor, environment, reward, failure semantics, and actor information boundary.
5. DRTP differs through bounded adaptive weighting of the six failure/topology groups.
6. Development 3M and held-out 10M are separate experimental strata.
7. Historical paired DRTP-minus-UTR return effects have positive means and medians, but dispersion is large.
8. Held-out seed2002 is a genuine adverse training-seed outcome and must be retained.
9. Safety is mixed rather than uniformly improved.
10. No fair frozen-contract external drop-in comparator was identified.

## D. Prohibited inference

The canon does not support:

- stable or consistently superior DRTP;
- universal topology robustness;
- recovery or information-restoration claims;
- causal attribution of seed sensitivity to a policy basin or a specific RNG source;
- hardware efficiency superiority;
- 4/5-UAV, real-time deployment, HIL, or real-flight generalization.
