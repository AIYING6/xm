# 07 Style Guide

## Stance

Scientific-question-driven, evidence-forward, and conservative about reliability. State the strongest result, then immediately state the seed and safety boundary.

## Paragraph rule

One paragraph performs one job: context, gap, method, result, comparison, interpretation, or limitation. Results paragraphs separate observations from causal interpretation.

## Quantitative wording

- Use “higher/lower than UTR under the stated contract” with metric and seed scope.
- Use “positive historical mean/median paired effect” for the mixed-contract summary.
- Reserve “significant” for a fully specified statistical test; otherwise use “large,” “positive,” “negative,” or “directionally consistent” with values.
- Never use episode count as the method-comparison `n`.

## Reliability wording

Preferred:

- “non-negligible training-seed sensitivity”;
- “four of five seeds favored DRTP in OOD worst, while seed2002 showed a severe reversal”;
- “descriptive cross-contract summary”;
- “mixed safety outcome.”

Forbidden:

- stable, reliably superior, consistently outperforms;
- universally robust or generalizes to unseen topologies;
- outlier when referring to seed2002;
- policy-basin divergence as an established mechanism;
- statistically significant without a defined test and independent unit.

## Legacy terminology

Do not use EA-RG-MAPPO-S, Full, Role-Gate, Gate Prior, recovery rate, RMST, kill-chain recovery, or information restoration as evidence for the DRTP manuscript.

## Citation placeholders

Unverified literature statements use `[CITATION NEEDED]`. Do not create a citation from memory. Method/result claims use a local source anchor until the final bibliography is verified.
