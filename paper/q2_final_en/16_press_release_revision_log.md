# Press-Release Revision Log

## Purpose

This revision applies the anti-defensive-writing principle to the English A-line
manuscript without weakening any required scientific disclosure. The manuscript
is organized around its strongest verified contribution: a controlled,
parameter- and exposure-matched formal comparison in which DRTP improves the
three primary relay-failure mission-score endpoints across all five paired
training seeds.

## Permitted narrative changes

| Change | Rationale | Evidence boundary retained |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Removed training-cohort sensitivity from the title | The title now names the method and application problem, not a limitation. | The abstract, Results 5.7, Discussion 6.2, Conclusion, and Supplementary S4 still disclose the independent-cohort direction. |
| Reordered the abstract's independent outcome | Formal matched evidence now leads the result paragraph. | The independent cohort and catastrophic seed remain reported with their frozen status. |
| Reframed the conclusion | The final sentence now ends with the paper's reusable contribution. | It retains the cross-cohort reliability boundary and does not claim stable superiority. |

## Non-negotiable disclosures

1. The independent 2401--2405 cohort is not pooled with 2301--2305.
2. The independent direction and catastrophic-seed observation remain in the
   abstract, results, discussion, conclusion, and Supplementary Information.
3. The primary endpoints are not called strict OOD endpoints, general
   robustness guarantees, or evidence that online adaptation is necessary.
4. B-line methods remain Supplementary S5 stress-test evidence and are not
   represented as successful alternatives or omitted to alter the primary
   DRTP--UTR estimate.
