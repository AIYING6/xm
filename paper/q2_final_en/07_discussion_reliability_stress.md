## Reliability stress tests and their boundary

To distinguish an unresolved reliability limitation from an untested implementation detail, we conducted exploratory stabilization stress tests after the formal DRTP comparison. These separate studies considered bounded sampler steps, a uniform sampling anchor, fixed KL-based actor protection, training-only paired probes, candidate selection, and a counterfactual critic. Some candidates improved a subset of development seeds, but none retained task gain, upper-tail performance, and reduced seed dispersion in its subsequent independent cohort. The counterfactual critic produced systematic performance degradation in two fresh five-seed cohorts. A compact protocol-level ledger is provided in Supplementary Table S5.

These post-formal studies are not alternative main methods, do not enter the estimate of the formal UTR--DRTP effect, and are not pooled across contracts. Their evidential role is narrower: they make it inappropriate to attribute the cohort reversal to a simple sampler excursion or to claim that a readily available local stabilizer has already resolved the issue. The observed training-cohort sensitivity is therefore retained as a boundary of Original DRTP rather than being hidden through selective reporting. The studies do not identify a single causal mechanism for that sensitivity, and they do not establish that stabilization of topology-adaptive MARL is impossible.
