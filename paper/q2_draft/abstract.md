# Abstract

Relay-node failures in heterogeneous multi-UAV missions can reorganize legal communication and task-support paths without implying a complete information blackout. We formulate this event as topology-perturbation robustness and study Distributionally Robust Topology-Perturbation SG-MAPPO (DRTP-SG-MAPPO). DRTP preserves the 116,728-parameter matched Single-Graph MAPPO backbone, PPO update, reward, environment, and decentralized actor information boundary. It changes only training exposure by retaining a 50% nominal competence anchor and applying bounded adaptive weights to six predefined perturbation groups. Against the matched Uniform Topology Randomization SG-MAPPO baseline, the historical audit reports higher average and median performance on F0, timing, duration, and compound conditions. At the 3M development endpoint, pooled F0, OOD-mean, and OOD-worst scores are 183.880, 183.464, and 172.241 for DRTP, compared with 127.929, 120.607, and 103.149 for UTR. The evidence is not seed-stable: development seed1902 and held-out seed2002 show adverse reversals, and safety improvements are not uniform. We therefore position DRTP as a high-upside but seed-sensitive topology-perturbation training strategy rather than a uniformly reliable robustness guarantee. The study reports absolute performance, paired seed-level effects, OOD decomposition, safety, risk-set trigger validity, and topology/path telemetry under separate frozen contracts.
